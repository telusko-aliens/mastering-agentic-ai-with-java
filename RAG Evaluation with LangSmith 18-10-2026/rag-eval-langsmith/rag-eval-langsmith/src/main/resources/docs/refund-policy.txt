NovaCart Refund and Returns Policy
Version 4.2, effective 1 September 2026

1. RETURN WINDOW

Most items may be returned within 30 days of delivery. The 30 days are counted from the
date the courier marks the parcel as delivered, not from the date the order was placed.

Large appliances and furniture have a 15 day return window, because of the cost of
collection. This shorter window is shown on the product page before the order is placed.

Items bought during a Black Friday or End of Season sale have an extended 45 day window,
and that extension applies even to discounted large appliances.

NovaCart Plus members get an extra 15 days on top of whatever window applies to the
item. A Plus member therefore has 45 days on an ordinary item and 30 days on a large
appliance.

2. CONDITION OF RETURNED ITEMS

An item must be returned unused, in its original packaging, with all accessories,
manuals and free gifts that came with it. A missing free gift is deducted from the
refund at its listed retail price.

Items with a broken manufacturer seal cannot be returned unless the item is faulty.

Clothing may be tried on for fit. It may not be worn outside, washed or altered. Tags
must still be attached.

3. ITEMS THAT CANNOT BE RETURNED

The following are final sale and cannot be returned or exchanged under any circumstances
except a manufacturing fault:

- Innerwear, swimwear and pierced jewellery
- Perishable food and fresh flowers
- Digital gift cards and downloadable software licences
- Custom engraved or made to order items
- Prescription eyewear and hearing aids
- Any item marked "Final Sale" on the product page at the time of purchase

4. HOW TO START A RETURN

Open the order in My Orders, choose Return or Replace, and pick a reason. A pickup is
scheduled within 2 business days in serviceable pin codes. Where pickup is not
available, NovaCart sends a prepaid shipping label and the customer drops the parcel at
any partner courier counter.

A return request can be cancelled by the customer at any time before the courier
collects the parcel.

If the courier fails to collect after three attempts, the return request is closed
automatically and the customer must raise a new one, provided the return window is still
open.

5. REFUND TIMELINES

Once the returned item reaches the NovaCart warehouse and passes inspection, the refund
is issued within 3 business days. Inspection itself takes up to 2 business days from the
day the warehouse receives the parcel.

The money then takes an additional amount of time to appear, depending on how the order
was paid:

- NovaCart Wallet: instant
- UPI: 1 to 2 business days
- Debit or credit card: 5 to 7 business days
- Net banking: 3 to 5 business days
- Cash on delivery: refunded to the bank account the customer supplies, 5 to 7 business
  days after the account details are verified

A refund to the NovaCart Wallet can be moved to a bank account afterwards, which takes a
further 2 business days.

6. REFUND AMOUNT

The refund covers the price actually paid for the item, including any tax.

The original delivery fee is refunded only when the return is caused by NovaCart: a
faulty item, a wrong item, or a damaged parcel. For a change of mind, the delivery fee
is not refunded.

Return pickup is free for faulty, wrong or damaged items. For a change of mind on an
item under 500 rupees, a pickup fee of 49 rupees is deducted from the refund.

If a discount code was applied across several items and only some are returned, the
discount is recalculated across the items that were kept, and the refund is the
difference. A customer is never asked to pay money back because of this recalculation.

Items bought with loyalty points are refunded in loyalty points, not cash. Points
returned this way keep their original expiry date.

7. FAULTY, WRONG OR DAMAGED ITEMS

Report a damaged parcel within 48 hours of delivery, with photographs of the packaging
and the item. Reported later, the claim is treated as a change of mind return and the
ordinary rules apply.

A faulty item can be reported at any point inside the return window. NovaCart will
replace it, or refund it in full including delivery, at the customer's choice.

An item that develops a fault after the return window has closed is a warranty matter,
not a return. The customer deals with the manufacturer, and NovaCart support will supply
the invoice and the warranty contact.

8. EXCHANGES

An exchange is available for a different size or colour of the same product, inside the
same window as a return, and only once per order line. For anything else, return the
item and place a new order.

If the replacement costs more than the original, the difference is charged before
dispatch. If it costs less, the difference is refunded by the ordinary timeline in
section 5.

9. CANCELLATIONS BEFORE DELIVERY

An order can be cancelled at no cost any time before it is dispatched. After dispatch
and before delivery, a cancellation is treated as a return and the ordinary refund rules
apply.

A customer who refuses a parcel at the door is treated the same way as a change of mind
return, including the delivery fee rule in section 6.

10. INTERNATIONAL ORDERS

Orders shipped outside India have a 21 day return window and the customer pays return
shipping in all cases, including faulty items. Customs duties already paid are not
refundable by NovaCart, and the customer must claim those from the relevant customs
authority directly.

11. ESCALATION

If a refund has not appeared 10 business days after the item passed inspection, contact
support with the order number. Support must respond within 1 business day and escalate
to the payments team if the delay is on NovaCart's side.

A complaint that is still unresolved 15 business days after escalation can be raised
with the NovaCart grievance officer, whose contact details are published in the Contact
Us section of the website.
